# Shatter Product And Technical Specification

## 1. Executive Summary

Shatter is a standalone disenchanting workflow addon for World of Warcraft TBC Classic / Anniversary. It gives enchanters a compact queue UI for finding disenchantable items, showing expected materials when available, guiding one disenchant action at a time, and tracking actual material output.

The MVP recreates the useful parts of the TradeSkillMaster Destroy UI for disenchanting without requiring TradeSkillMaster. Long term, Shatter supports three modes:

- Solo Mode: bag cleanup and normal personal disenchanting.
- Mail Mode: guided processing of mailed disenchant requests and material returns.
- Raid / Trade Mode: guided handling of raid loot-master or trade-based disenchant batches.

Shatter must respect Blizzard addon restrictions. It must not automate gameplay unattended, bypass protected actions, or hide action sequencing in the background. Every protected or user-sensitive action is presented as an explicit next step with visible state and a clear button.

The UX should be ElvUI-inspired and close to AltTracker: dark translucent panels, thin borders, clean typography, yellow active text, restrained cyan/blue accents, item quality colors, and native Classic-style frames. ElvUI must not be required.

Current repository state as of 2026-05-16:

- The repository is initialized for Shatter.
- `SPEC.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `LICENSE`, `.pkgmeta`, `.gitignore`, `Shatter.toc`, and a minimal `Core.lua` load scaffold exist.
- Functional addon behavior is not implemented yet.

## 2. Goals And Non-Goals

### Goals

- Provide a standalone disenchant queue UI for TBC Classic enchanters.
- Scan player bags for disenchantable items and show the next best item to process.
- Let the player disenchant one item per click through a safe guided action.
- Track actual materials generated by each disenchant.
- Separate expected material prediction from market value, so the addon remains useful with no pricing source.
- Support saved settings, ignored items, clear status messages, and debug logs.
- Later, support mail and raid/trade sessions with source attribution and material return summaries.
- Keep the addon lightweight, native, and easy to package for CurseForge.

### Non-Goals

- No unattended disenchanting, mail opening, mail sending, trading, or trade accepting.
- No background botting behavior.
- No protected-action bypasses.
- No dependency on TradeSkillMaster, Postal, Gargul, ElvUI, Ace, or other heavy frameworks.
- No Phase 1 auction-house scanning or market database.
- No promise of perfect material attribution when generated materials merge with existing inventory stacks. Shatter will maintain a logical session ledger and clearly explain uncertainty when needed.

## 3. User Personas / Workflows

### Solo Enchanter

The player finishes questing, dungeons, or crafting and wants to clean up green/blue items. They open Shatter, review the queue, optionally ignore or skip items, and click `Shatter Next` once per item. Shatter tracks the material result and shows a summary.

Success criteria:

- The player can safely process eligible items without searching bags manually.
- The player always knows which item is next.
- The addon never disenchants an item without a click.
- The addon can continue after failed casts, missing loot, bag updates, or a rescan.

### Mail Disenchant Enchanter

Guildmates mail items to the enchanter. At the mailbox, the player opens Shatter Mail Mode, selects included mails, takes attachments through a guided flow, disenchants the items, and returns generated materials to each sender.

Success criteria:

- Shatter tracks which sender each source item came from.
- Mail operations handle delays and reindexing.
- Materials are attributed to the original sender through the active session ledger.
- The player explicitly sends or confirms each outgoing mail.

### Raid / Trade Enchanter

In a raid, a loot master or raid member trades green/blue items to the enchanter. Shatter records the trade intake, adds those items to a raid session queue, tracks generated materials, and helps trade materials back to the loot master or assigned recipient.

Success criteria:

- Shatter records trade partner, exact traded items, and completed trade confirmation.
- Raid materials are kept separate from normal solo processing in the session model.
- Return trade is guided and never auto-accepted.

## 4. MVP Scope

Phase 1 MVP is Solo Mode only.

### Included

- `/shatter` slash command to show/hide the main frame.
- `/shatter sim` and `/shatter simreset` development commands for no-destroy queue testing.
- Compact native main frame with mode tabs, queue, selected item details, result panel, and settings entry.
- Bag scan for eligible disenchantable items.
- Queue of eligible items with icon, item link/name, quality color, item level, source bag/slot, expected materials if known, and status.
- Settings:
  - Maximum item quality to disenchant, default `Rare`.
  - Include soulbound items, default `false`.
  - Minimum expected value threshold, default `0`.
  - Ignored items list.
- Debug logging, default `false`.
- Development simulation mode, default `false`, for recording fake disenchant results without casting or consuming items during implementation/testing.
- `Shatter Next` primary button.
- Secure guided disenchant action for one selected bag item per click.
- Result tracking through loot events and bag snapshots.
- Session summary for items disenchanted, materials generated, skipped items, failed items, and unknown results.
- Safe expected-results fallback when material tables or pricing are unavailable.

### Excluded From MVP

- Mail Mode processing.
- Raid / Trade Mode processing.
- Auction value integration.
- Profiles.
- Minimap button unless it proves useful after the main frame exists.
- Automated stack combining.
- Permanent cross-session recovery beyond basic saved settings and debug history.

- Active text: WoW gold/yellow.
- Secondary accent: restrained cyan/blue for informational values.
- Item rows: item quality color for item name; subtle row highlight on hover/selection.
- Fonts: native `GameFontNormal`, `GameFontHighlightSmall`, and related Classic fonts.

### Main Layout

- Title bar: centered `Shatter`, close button at the top right.
- Mode tabs: compact segmented buttons for `Solo`, `Mail`, `Raid`.
- Queue panel: scrollable item rows.
- Detail panel:
  - Large item icon.
  - Item link/name.
  - Quality, item level, bind state, bag/slot.
  - Expected materials.
  - Actual result after completion.
- Footer/status:
  - Status text on the left.
  - Primary action button on the right.
  - Secondary buttons near the primary action.

- `Shatter Next`: ready to disenchant the selected/next item.

- `Trade Mats`: Raid Mode has materials to add to a trade.

- `Ignore Item`: adds selected item ID to the permanent ignore list.
- `Skip`: skips the selected queue entry for the current session.
- `Stop Session`: stops active Mail/Raid workflow and records partial summary.
- `Summary`: opens current or latest session summary.

### Status Text

Required statuses:

- `Ready`
- `No eligible items`

- `Inventory full`
- `Missing enchanting profession`
- `Mailbox required`
- `Trade window required`

### Material Tracking Model

Primary attribution should use loot events:

- When Shatter begins a disenchant, set `session.pendingAction` to the queue item.
- On `LOOT_READY`, read each loot slot with `GetLootSlotLink` and `GetLootSlotInfo`.
- Attribute loot slot materials to `pendingAction.sourceId`.
- On `LOOT_CLOSED` plus delayed bag update, validate that the source item stack changed.

Fallback attribution uses bag snapshots:

- Before action: count tracked enchanting materials in all bags.
- After action: count again after `LOOT_CLOSED` and bag update.
- Delta becomes the result if loot slots were unavailable.
- If output merged with existing stacks, attribution remains logical, not physical.

## 8. State Machines

### Solo Mode

States:

- `IDLE`: frame hidden or no active session.
- `SCANNING`: scanning bags and rebuilding queue.
- `READY`: queue has at least one valid item and the button is enabled.
- `CASTING`: secure button has been clicked and Shatter is waiting for spellcast start/success/failure.
- `WAITING_LOOT`: spell completed and loot/bag events are expected.
- `RECORDING_RESULT`: material results are attached to the pending queue item.
- `COMPLETE`: no more eligible queued items.
- `ERROR`: workflow is blocked until rescan, skip, or user correction.

Transitions:

- `IDLE -> SCANNING`: `/shatter`, frame show, settings change, or bag update.
- `SCANNING -> READY`: at least one eligible item.
- `SCANNING -> COMPLETE`: no eligible items.
- `READY -> CASTING`: user clicks `Shatter Next`.
- `CASTING -> WAITING_LOOT`: player spellcast succeeds.
- `CASTING -> ERROR`: spellcast fails, item missing, profession missing, or macro target invalid.
- `WAITING_LOOT -> RECORDING_RESULT`: loot and bag events arrive.
- `RECORDING_RESULT -> SCANNING`: result recorded, queue refresh needed.
- `ERROR -> SCANNING`: user clicks rescan or bag state changes.

### Mail Mode

States:

- `MAIL_IDLE`: no active mail workflow.
- `MAIL_SCAN`: mailbox open, scanning inbox headers and attachments.
- `MAIL_SELECT`: user selects included mails.
- `TAKE_READY`: one selected attachment is ready to take.
- `TAKING_ATTACHMENT`: Shatter called the mailbox API from a user action and is waiting for update.
- `WAIT_MAIL_UPDATE`: waiting for `MAIL_INBOX_UPDATE`, bag update, or timeout/backoff.
- `DISENCHANT_QUEUE`: selected items are in the normal guided disenchant queue.
- `RETURN_PREP`: materials are ready to return by sender.
- `WAIT_USER_SEND`: outgoing mail is prepared and user must send or confirm.
- `MAIL_SUMMARY`: workflow complete or stopped.
- `MAIL_ERROR`: mailbox closed, bag full, mail changed, COD skipped, or attachment unavailable.

Rules:

- Mail Mode requires the mailbox UI to be open.
- Do not process all selected mail unattended.
- The primary button should take one attachment or prepare one outgoing mail step per click unless Phase 0 proves a bounded sequence is safe and still visibly user-initiated.
- Always rescan/re-match the current mail row before taking an attachment.
- Skip COD, GM, returned, or money-only mails unless future settings explicitly allow them.
- If bags are full, stop and show `Inventory full`.
- If mail reindexes, rebuild fingerprints and resume only when the intended mail can be confidently matched.

### Raid / Trade Mode

States:

- `RAID_IDLE`: no active raid workflow.
- `TRADE_OPEN`: trade frame open and state snapshots are being updated.
- `TRADE_SNAPSHOT`: trade accept changed and both sides are captured.
- `TRADE_COMPLETE`: completed trade confirmed by `ERR_TRADE_COMPLETE`.
- `DISENCHANT_QUEUE`: received items are queued.
- `RETURN_READY`: materials are ready to return.
- `WAIT_TRADE`: waiting for correct recipient trade window.
- `TRADE_MATS_READY`: one material stack is ready to add.
- `WAIT_USER_ACCEPT`: trade contents are prepared; user confirms through native trade UI.
- `RAID_SUMMARY`: workflow complete or stopped.
- `RAID_ERROR`: trade cancelled, wrong partner, recipient missing, or unresolved materials.

Rules:

- Snapshot target trade items before completion.
- Commit intake only after the trade-complete message.
- Do not accept or confirm trade automatically.
- If adding items to a trade is allowed, do it as a guided visible action and never as a hidden loop.
- If recipient is not nearby, offer chat announce/whisper guidance instead of trying to force trade.

## 9. Event/API Research Checklist

Phase 0 must validate all APIs in the live TBC Anniversary client before Phase 1 implementation.

### Disenchant And Bags

- Confirm `IsSpellKnown(13262)` correctly identifies Disenchant.
- Confirm Enchanting profession detection through available profession APIs and/or spellbook scan.
- Confirm whether `C_Container.*` or legacy `GetContainer*` APIs are correct for bags in Interface `20505`.
- Confirm secure button behavior for:
  - `/cast Disenchant`
  - `/use bag slot`
  - Macro text set during `PreClick` or before click.
- Confirm event order for successful disenchant:
  - `UNIT_SPELLCAST_START`
  - `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED`
  - `LOOT_READY`
  - `LOOT_CLOSED`
  - bag update events
- Confirm event order for failed cast, item too high skill, moving, combat, and item missing.
- Confirm if `GetLootSlotLink` and `GetLootSlotInfo` reliably expose disenchant materials.

### Disenchant Eligibility

Research approach:

- Use item quality, item class, item level, and known disenchant tables.
- Include armor and weapon classes only unless validated otherwise.
- Exclude poor/common items.
- Exclude items above settings max quality.
- Exclude soulbound items unless enabled.
- Exclude denylisted non-disenchantable item IDs.
- Require sufficient Enchanting skill if a required skill can be derived from item level tables.

Known hard part:

- There may be no single native `CanDisenchantItem` API in TBC Classic.
- Some items look eligible by class/quality but cannot be disenchanted.
- Shatter needs a denylist and a failure path that records `not disenchantable` without retry loops.

### Mailbox

- Confirm `GetInboxNumItems`, `GetInboxHeaderInfo`, `GetInboxItem`, and `GetInboxItemLink` return shape in TBC Anniversary.
- Confirm `TakeInboxItem` behavior, event order, and throttling.
- Confirm `TakeInboxMoney` behavior, even if Shatter initially skips money mail.
- Confirm `SendMailNameEditBox`, `SendMailSubjectEditBox`, `SendMailBodyEditBox`, `ClickSendMailItemButton`, and `SendMail` behavior.
- Confirm whether `SendMail` can be called from a Shatter hardware click or must be left to the native Send button.
- Confirm `MAIL_SHOW`, `MAIL_CLOSED`, `MAIL_INBOX_UPDATE`, `MAIL_SEND_SUCCESS`, `UI_ERROR_MESSAGE`, and any failure event names.
- Confirm mailbox reindex behavior after taking attachments.
- Validate delays and backoff. Postal-style behavior waits for mailbox item/money counts to change instead of assuming instant success.

### Trade

- Confirm `TRADE_SHOW`, `TRADE_CLOSED`, `TRADE_ACCEPT_UPDATE`, `TRADE_PLAYER_ITEM_CHANGED`, `TRADE_TARGET_ITEM_CHANGED`, and `TRADE_REQUEST_CANCEL`.
- Confirm trade completion signal through `UI_INFO_MESSAGE` and `ERR_TRADE_COMPLETE`.
- Confirm partner naming using `UnitName("NPC", true)` and `TradeFrameRecipientNameText`.
- Confirm item snapshots with `GetTradeTargetItemLink`, `GetTradeTargetItemInfo`, `GetTradePlayerItemLink`, and `GetTradePlayerItemInfo`.
- Confirm `InitiateTrade` usability from a button.
- Confirm whether adding bag items to trade by `UseContainerItem` is allowed from a guided click.
- Confirm `AcceptTrade` protection. Treat native trade accept as user-controlled even if a function exists.

- Secure action buttons: https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/SecureActionButtonTemplate
- Trade accept protection: https://addonstudio.org/wiki/WoW:API_AcceptTrade
- Trade events: https://addonstudio.org/wiki/WoW:Events/Trade

- Central event frame.

### Session.lua

- Owns active session state machine.

- Creates summaries.
- Stops sessions cleanly.

### MaterialTracker.lua

- Counts material snapshots.
- Reads loot slots.
- Computes deltas.
- Updates per-source ledgers.
- Flags uncertain attribution.

### UI Modules

- `UI/MainFrame.lua`: shell, tabs, status, primary action.
- `UI/Rows.lua`: reusable scroll rows for queues, mail, summaries.

- `Modes/SoloMode.lua`: Phase 1 behavior.
- `Modes/MailMode.lua`: Phase 3 behavior.
- `Modes/RaidMode.lua`: Phase 4 behavior.

### Integrations

- `Integrations/AuctionData.lua`: optional price providers. Must fail closed and return nil when unavailable.

### Debug.lua

- Prefix logs with `Shatter`.
- Support severity levels: `debug`, `info`, `warn`, `error`.

## 12. Error Handling And Recovery

### General Errors

- Missing Enchanting: disable primary button and show `Missing enchanting profession`.
- Disenchant not known: disable primary button and show `Missing Disenchant`.
- Item moved: rescan and show `Item moved or missing`.
- Item no longer eligible: remove from queue and record skipped reason.
- Inventory full: pause and show `Inventory full`.
- Loot missing: record unknown result and allow continue.
- Combat lockdown: if secure frame changes are blocked, defer action setup until safe and show blocked status.

### Mail Errors

- Mailbox closed: pause Mail Mode and show `Mailbox required`.
- Mail reindexed: rescan and re-match by fingerprint.
- Mail cannot be matched: mark mail unresolved and require user review.
- Attachment missing: mark skipped.
- COD mail: skip by default.
- Bag full: stop taking attachments.
- Send failed: keep ledger outstanding and show sender/materials unresolved.

### Raid Errors

- Trade cancelled: discard uncommitted trade snapshot.
- Wrong trade partner during return: show warning and do not add mats.
- Recipient not nearby: offer announce/whisper guidance.
- Trade complete missing confirmation: do not commit intake.
- Reload mid-session: Phase 4 may restore logical ledger, but exact bag-slot tracking must be revalidated.

### Recovery Policy

- Phase 1: recover through rescan and summary only.
- Phase 3/4: persist active session ledger where feasible, but require explicit user review after reload or disconnect.
- Never resume destructive or protected actions automatically after reload.
